Yamabuki Ryokan — Hot Spring Inn in the Mountains of Gunma

A quiet 12-room ryokan in the mountains of Gunma, with natural hot spring baths and dinner and breakfast served in your room. English-speaking staff at the front desk.

Your stay

  • Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 10:00.
  • Rates: from ¥28,000 per person per night, including dinner and breakfast.
  • Children: ages 6–12 stay at 50% of the adult rate (with meals). Children under 6 stay free when sharing a futon with an adult (no meals).
  • Meals: seasonal kaiseki dinner and Japanese breakfast. Please tell us about any food allergies at least 3 days before your arrival.

Hot spring baths

  • Two indoor baths and one open-air bath, open 15:00–24:00 and 6:00–9:30.
  • A private bath can be reserved for 45 minutes at no extra charge. Reservation required; please ask at check-in.
  • Guests with tattoos are welcome to use the private bath. Tattoos are not permitted in the shared baths.

Access

  • Free pick-up from the nearest train station (about 20 minutes by car). Reservation required; please tell us your arrival time the day before.
  • By car: free parking for 12 cars. Snow tires are needed from December to March.

Good to know

  • Payment: cash, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express), and bank transfer in advance.
  • Cancellation: 50% of the room rate the day before your arrival, 100% on the day of arrival or for no-shows.
  • Wi-Fi is available in all rooms. Yukata and towels are provided.
