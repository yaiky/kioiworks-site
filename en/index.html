Kagami Glass Studio — Edo Kiriko Cut-Glass Workshop in Tokyo

Cut your own glass in a 90-minute hands-on class, guided by a craftsman with 30 years of experience. No Japanese needed.

What you'll do

  • Choose your glass (a tumbler or a sake cup) and a pattern from six traditional designs.
  • Learn to hold the glass against the spinning wheel and cut the lines yourself. Your instructor guides each cut.
  • Take your finished piece home the same day, wrapped for travel.

Good to know

  • Duration: 90 minutes. Classes start at 10:30 and 14:00.
  • Price: ¥6,500 per person (tax included). Cash and credit cards accepted.
  • Ages 10 and up. Children under 15 must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Instructions are given in English with a printed guide. Our staff speak basic English; for anything else, ask our chat assistant below.
  • Wear closed shoes and short sleeves. Aprons and safety glasses are provided.
  • Cancellation: free up to 24 hours before your class.

Access

  • 8 minutes on foot from Kinshicho Station (JR Sobu Line / Hanzomon Line). Look for the blue noren curtain.
  • Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–18:00. Closed Mondays.
